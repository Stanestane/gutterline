Controls

Gutter
Drag to move. Drag near either end to angle it. Double-click to straighten.
Panel
Click to select. Double-click or drop a file to load an image.
Overlap
Drag the edge of an overlapping panel to move it. Drag a corner to reshape it, one corner at a time.
Image
Drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Parts outside the frame are hidden.
Keys
Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Shift+Z redo, Delete removes the image, Alt while dragging turns off snapping.
